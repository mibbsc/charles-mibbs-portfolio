MY STORY / DRAFT

Discipline meets curiosity.

Before moving into technology, I served in the U.S. Army as an infantryman, including responsibilities as an automatic rifleman and team leader. That experience taught me to prepare deliberately, communicate clearly, stay accountable to the team, and take details seriously—especially when timing matters.

I’m now studying Cloud and Network Engineering at Western Governors University on the AWS track. I’m building my skills through structured coursework and hands-on practice with Linux, networking, and cloud concepts. I enjoy taking something unfamiliar, breaking it into smaller problems, and working through it until I understand how it fits together.

My next goal is to earn a remote-friendly internship where I can contribute, learn from experienced engineers, and turn classroom knowledge into practical experience. Longer term, I plan to continue into cybersecurity and help build systems that are dependable, maintainable, and secure.

This site is my living workbench: a place to document what I’m learning, show the projects I build, and make it easy for people to connect with me.
